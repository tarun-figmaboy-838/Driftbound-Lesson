# Lines & Angles: flight training before Driftbound

The lesson students take before playing [Driftbound](https://zathura-teal.vercel.app). It follows the
"Lines & Angles · Class 2" deck (*Angles Learning section.pdf*) slide by slide: how an angle is formed,
angle types, complementary pairs, adjacent angles, the linear pair axiom and its converse, vertically
opposite angles and their proof, and the recall. On the last step, Launch mission opens the game.

It opens on a start banner (the Driftbound title over the satellite HQ in orbit, and a play button).
Play runs a short intro film: into the satellite, through its gate, down the corridor and round the
command hall, where a drone projects the lesson's screen. The lesson plays on that one big hologram
screen. The narration is one line at the top, read aloud by a recorded voice (the game's co-pilot,
shown as an AI orb); each word lights up as it is said. In the recall, a drone projects the five
summary cards one by one.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript modules: no build step, no packages. The design of the lesson (flow,
mapping to the deck and to the game) is in [docs/lesson-design.md](docs/lesson-design.md).

## Run it

On a Mac, double-click `start.command`: it starts a local server and opens the lesson in your browser.
Or run `python3 serve.py` and open http://127.0.0.1:8124.

It must be served (opened from `file://`, the browser blocks the modules; Play then says so). The
fonts load from Google Fonts. `?step=12` opens a given step directly, skipping the banner and intro.
`?dev=1` adds a jump list in the top corner to open any step (past locked Next); a reload stays on it.

## How it fits together

- **A step** (in `src/lesson/steps.js`) is an `async run(c)` script. `c` is the toolkit from
  `makeCtx()` in `src/main.js`: `c.caption()`, `c.say()`, `c.view()` (a figure with state),
  `c.choose()` / `c.cards()` (questions with an explanation for every wrong answer and a line on what
  was right), `c.ready()`
  (unlocks Next). Leaving a step cancels its script.
- **The narration** shows one sentence at a time; every line it can say has a recording in
  `assets/vo`, found by a hash of the exact text. Change a line's text and it needs a new recording.
- **Assets** are one file each: pictures are WebP, the voice is AAC (.m4a). Both play in every browser
  the lesson supports, so there are no fallback copies.

## Changing things

- **A line of narration:** edit it in `src/lesson/steps.js`, then remake its voice. With Python
  3.10-3.12 and `pip install kokoro soundfile scipy numpy`, run `python tools/vo/make_vo.py`
  (it keeps unchanged lines and removes files for lines that are gone; `--list` only prints them).
  Until then the line shows without a voice. A long line can be split with `tools/vo/split_vo.py`.
- **A picture:** `python3 tools/ui/encode_images.py source.png assets/ui/<area>/<name>.webp`.
- **The background:** after replacing `assets/ui/bg/`, update `PICS` in `src/engine/scene.js` (the
  picture's size and where the lesson screen goes on its front wall, in picture pixels).
- **The buttons' art:** replace the art in `tools/ui/polygon/` and run `python3 tools/ui/inline_pills.py`.
- **The game link:** `GAME_URL` in `src/main.js`.
